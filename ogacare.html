Competitor Watch — Indian Clinic EMR Market

Benchmarking OGA Care (ogacare.in) against the clinic/EMR field · compiled 13 Sep 2026

6 competitors tracked

Threat snapshot

TatvaCare Primary threat

TatvaPractice — ABDM-certified EMR + practice management

Advantage
NHA-approved, ABDM-certified with native ABHA creation in the consult flow; HIPAA-aligned, ISO 27001, DPDPA-compliant; HL7/FHIR + HIS/lab/pharmacy connectors; markets itself as "India's fastest-growing EMR."
Threat to OGA Care
Closest positioning match — small clinic to hospital EMR with certified ABDM depth OGA Care doesn't yet publicly claim. Active content engine pushing ABDM-urgency messaging to convert undecided clinics.
Gaps we can exploit
Reviews flag setup/integration friction; AI is not the headline feature — positioning leans compliance-first, not AI-native.

Eka Care High

"AI-native Health OS" — EMR, ABDM Connect, patient PHR app

Advantage
Deepest ABHA/PHR consumer network of the group (patient-side app + doctor EMR), EkaScribe voice-to-note AI, DocAssist clinical guidance, vernacular/multilingual prescriptions, WhatsApp-native follow-ups.
Threat to OGA Care
Two-sided network effect (patients + doctors) is hard to out-market; AI-first brand competes directly on OGA Care's "AI-native" claim.
Gaps we can exploit
Value pitch leans patient/record aggregation over deep single-clinic customization; third-party comparisons note it's strongest where the patient's own doctors are already on Eka.

Practo Ray Medium

Clinic module of Practo's doctor-discovery marketplace

Advantage
Largest patient-discovery funnel in India; ABHA sync pushes records to patients' digital locker automatically; WhatsApp/SMS reminders, integrated payments.
Threat to OGA Care
Distribution muscle, not product depth — clinics adopt Ray to be discoverable, not for clinical AI.
Gaps we can exploit
Reviewers consistently flag no AI documentation, basic billing, limited IPD, and vendor lock-in to the Practo ecosystem — directly opposite OGA Care's AI-assistant + custom-branding pitch.

MocDoc Medium

HMS/EMR — OPD to hospital scale, IVF/fertility niche strength

Advantage
Full OPD+IPD+pharmacy+lab+billing suite; AIIMS/MoHFW guideline-driven prescription suggestions; ABDM/HFR-oriented for NABH pathways; strong in specialty niches (fertility/IVF).
Threat to OGA Care
Scale advantage in hospital/multi-department accounts — a path upmarket OGA Care doesn't compete on today.
Gaps we can exploit
Built for facility-wide HMS, not the fast solo-doctor onboarding OGA Care leads with; heavier implementation.

KareXpert Low (for now)

Jio Platforms-funded enterprise HIMS (HMS+EMR+LIMS+PACS+telemedicine)

Advantage
Deep-pocketed backer, 500+ hospitals across 70+ cities, full stack including PACS/RIS and connected-ambulance modules — genuine enterprise scale.
Threat to OGA Care
Not a direct competitor for solo doctors/small clinics; watch for a down-market push given Jio's distribution reach.
Gaps / caution
Sources disagree on live ABDM status — one 2023 vendor release claims certification complete, a mid-2026 comparison site says ABDM is "on the roadmap, not live" and SOC 2 Type II is still in progress. Verify before citing in any client-facing comparison.

Clinicea Low

Highly customizable specialty EMR, 20+ specialties, global footprint

Advantage
Best-in-class customization — converts paper case sheets into bespoke digital EMR per clinic; strong multilingual prescription/invoice customization; loyal aesthetics/IVF/multi-branch client base.
Threat to OGA Care
Competes on customization depth, a space OGA Care also claims via specialty-aware templates — but Clinicea's is white-glove/bespoke, not self-serve.
Gaps we can exploit
No prominent India-specific ABDM/ABHA messaging found — sales-led, custom pricing, slower self-serve onboarding than OGA Care's same-day setup.

What to do next

Close the ABDM/ABHA gap

TatvaCare, Eka Care, Practo Ray and MocDoc all lead with certified or well-documented ABHA workflows. OGA Care's public site doesn't yet show this — highest-priority messaging and/or roadmap item.

Publish multilingual Rx support

Only Eka Care and Clinicea visibly market vernacular/multilingual prescriptions. If OGA Care already supports this, it isn't showing on the marketing site — easy differentiation if true.

Lean into AI + offline + same-day onboarding

OGA Care's combination of AI-native drafting, offline-safe billing and same-day setup isn't matched by any single competitor here — Eka has AI, TatvaCare has compliance, Clinicea has customisation, but none combine all three with OGA Care's onboarding speed.
